Block Off Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Block Off is a puzzle game by Enversion Labs ("we") that you can play without an account. We do not collect or store any personal information about you. The game does show advertising, and the advertising provider collects some information to do so. This policy explains both.

Information we collect

None. Block Off has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics of our own. We do not receive, store or sell any personal data.

Advertising

Block Off shows adverts provided by Google AdMob: a banner during play, and optional video adverts that you can choose to watch in return for a reward.

To deliver and measure adverts, Google may collect and process information from your device, including:

  • your device's advertising identifier
  • your IP address, which indicates your approximate location
  • device information, such as model and operating system version
  • how you interact with adverts

Google uses this information as described in its own policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Your choices

  • Consent. Where the law requires it, such as in the UK and the European Economic Area, you are asked whether you agree to personalised advertising the first time you open the game. If you decline, you still see adverts, but they are not based on your personal data. You can change your choice at any time by tapping Privacy choices on the game's home screen or pause menu.
  • iOS. You can control whether apps may track you under Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Tracking.
  • Android. You can reset or delete your advertising identifier under Settings, then Privacy, then Ads.

Information stored on your device

The game saves the following on your own device so that you can carry on where you left off:

  • your current game in progress
  • your best score
  • your sound and vibration preferences

This information is not sent to us. It is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Children

Block Off is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same address with a new date.
